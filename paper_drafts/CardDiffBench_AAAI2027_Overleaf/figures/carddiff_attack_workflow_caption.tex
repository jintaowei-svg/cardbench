\caption{How a CardDiff attack unfolds in \textsc{A2ACardBench}. A test case generator combines an attack vector $a$, application domain $d$, task $t$, and schema-valid perturbation $\delta$ to construct public and active AgentCard views that differ only in attack-relevant control-plane fields. The A2A Host admits the retrieved or cached card state, selects a skill and interface with a binding/version, and applies authorization and output-mode decisions. The resulting message dispatch and artifact events form trace $\tau$, which is evaluated by the attack-specific oracle $\Phi_a$. Panel (b) maps the seven active vectors to state admission (A1--A3), control-plane selection (B1 and B2), and policy enforcement (C1 and C2). Attack success rate measures the specified control-plane event; downstream impact rate is evaluated separately.}
